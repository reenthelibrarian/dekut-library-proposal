Welcome to DeKUT University Library

Welcome to Dedan Kimathi University of Technology University Library. Dedan Kimathi University of Technology University Library is one of the few Universities in the country that is privileged to have adopted and enjoying a wide range of innovative services and systems that are used to necessitate easy access and maximum utilization of library information resources.

The library’s primary responsibility is to assist students and academic staff in the process of transforming information into knowledge; it does this by providing information that is essential for the purpose of teaching, learning, innovation and research. The Library has wide access to information materials that are geared to support teaching, learning, research, and innovation activities for students, academic staff, researchers and the community at large. Currently, the library provides access to approximately 60,000 volumes of print resources, 20,000 databases of electronic journals and over 500,000 electronic books.

Dedan Kimathi University Library operates from the Main Campus Library, which comprises two branches serving all DeKUT bonafide students, staff, and researchers. As an information center, we are pleased to serve you and assist you in all your academic endeavors.
